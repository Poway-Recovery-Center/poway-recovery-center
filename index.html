Visiting for the first time?

  • You don't need to sign up or explain your story to attend a meeting.
  • "Open" meetings welcome anyone. "Closed" meetings are for people who want to stop drinking or using.
  • Some meetings are upstairs. Each meeting's room and access details are listed on the schedule.
  • Hybrid meetings also run on Zoom. The Zoom details are listed with each meeting.
