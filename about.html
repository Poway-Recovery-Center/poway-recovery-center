Who we are

Poway Recovery Center is a meeting space run by and for people in recovery. Groups gather here every day of the week, from early-morning discussion meetings to evening speaker meetings, in person and on Zoom.

We pair peer connection with practical help: finding treatment, crisis support, housing, food and transportation. We don't promise a perfect path. We promise to treat you with honesty, respect and a focus on safety.

What we believe

Dignity

Every person deserves to be treated as a whole human being, not a diagnosis or a bad day.

Choice

Support should add to your sense of control, not take it away. You decide what help looks like.

Connection

Recovery is easier with people around you. Nobody has to do this alone.
