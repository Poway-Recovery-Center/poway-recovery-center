How reserving works

  • Reserving is optional. Walk-ins are always welcome.
  • You can hold up to 5 spots at a meeting if you're bringing people.
  • Spots open 30 days ahead and close when the meeting starts.
  • Cancel any time before the meeting if your plans change.
